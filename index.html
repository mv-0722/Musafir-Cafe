Welcome to Musafir

A café for wanderers, dreamers & food lovers.

Musafir means traveller. Our cafe is inspired by the little discoveries that make a journey memorable — an unexpected chai stall, a plate of hot samosas, music from a nearby radio and conversations with complete strangers.

We bring those moments together under one roof, with recipes inspired by kitchens from across India.

Our story

Every traveller has a story.

Ours began with three friends, a backpack, a railway platform and a love for chai.

Since 2021

Inspired by India's roads.

Musafir Cafe started as a simple idea: recreate the feeling of discovering a beautiful roadside cafe during a long journey.

From Assam's tea gardens to the old lanes of Delhi, from Mumbai's bustling food stalls to Rajasthan's colourful kitchens, every region gave us something worth bringing home.

Today, Musafir is our little tribute to those journeys — served one plate and one cup at a time.

Made with heart

Fresh ingredients, house masalas and recipes that respect their roots.

Earth conscious

Kulhads, local ingredients and thoughtful choices wherever possible.

Slow living

Acoustic Fridays, poetry Sundays and evenings without a clock.
